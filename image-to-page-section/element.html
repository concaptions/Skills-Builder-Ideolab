Staircases, made to the opening

Measured in your house, cut in our workshop

No two openings are the same, and a staircase ordered from a catalogue has to be packed out on site to fit one. We survey the opening first, draw it, and cut the strings and treads to that drawing.

The stair arrives in three pieces and goes in over a day and a half. You get the workshop drawing before anything is cut.

  • Surveyed before quoting. The price on the quote is the price on the invoice.
  • Oak, ash or painted softwood. All of it cut in the same workshop, by the same two joiners.
