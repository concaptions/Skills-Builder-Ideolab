01About Our Brand

About us

We started because nobody turned up

In 2008 the founder waited three weeks for a plumber who never came, fixed the leak himself, and decided that turning up on the day you said you would was a low enough bar to build a business on.

Eighteen years later there are eleven of us. The van is newer and the workshop is bigger, but the rule has not changed: we say a date, and we are there on it.

We still answer our own phone. If you call at four on a Friday, somebody who knows your job picks up.

05Expandable Content

Questions

The things people ask first

If your question is not here, send it over and you will get a straight answer the same day.

How quickly can you start?

Small jobs are usually booked within the same week. Anything that needs materials ordering runs to about two weeks, and you get the date in writing before we start.

Is the quote fixed, or does it move?

Fixed. The price you agree is the price you pay. If we find something genuinely unexpected behind a wall we stop, show you, and agree the cost before carrying on.

Do you clear up afterwards?

Every time. Dust sheets go down before the first tool comes out, and waste leaves with us on the day rather than sitting in your driveway.

What if something goes wrong later?

Workmanship is guaranteed for two years and parts carry the manufacturer's own cover. One phone call and we come back out, at no charge.

Which areas do you cover?

Anywhere within about forty minutes of the workshop. If you are slightly outside that, ask anyway, because it often still works out.
