What is included, and how it runs

A tick where the order does not matter. A number where it does.

Included in every quote

  • The boiler, the flue and every fitting the job needs
  • Taking the old unit away and disposing of it
  • A system flush and every radiator balanced
  • Registering the warranty in your name before we leave
  • Clearing up, including the bits you cannot see

How the day runs

  1. 1 You send a photograph of the old boiler and the meter
  2. 2 We price it in writing, with nothing added later
  3. 3 An engineer arrives on the agreed morning
  4. 4 The system is flushed, balanced and tested with you there
  5. 5 The warranty is registered and the paperwork is emailed
