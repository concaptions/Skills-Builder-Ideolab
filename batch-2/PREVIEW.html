02Text Paragraph

A boiler that is sized properly costs less to run every winter it is fitted, and most of the ones we replace were never sized at all.

The survey takes about forty minutes. We measure the rooms, count the radiators and look at the pipework you already have, then quote against that rather than against a guess. The number on the quote is the number you pay, and if we find something behind a wall that changes the job we stop and tell you before we carry on.

Most replacements are finished inside a day, and the heating is back on the same evening.
