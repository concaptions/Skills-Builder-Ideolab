What happens behind the wall

  1. 1

    The boiler is sized to the house

    Not to the one it replaced. Most of the boilers we take out were oversized when they went in, which is why they short cycle and cost more to run.

  2. 2

    The cylinder holds enough for two showers

    Back to back, without a cold run at the end of the second one. That figure comes from the number of bathrooms, not from a rule of thumb.

  3. 3

    The manifold isolates one room

    So a leak in the kitchen does not mean draining the whole system. It is a small extra on the day and saves a day later.

  4. 4

    Every radiator is balanced on the day

    Commissioned room by room before we leave, so the far bedroom warms up at the same rate as the hall.
