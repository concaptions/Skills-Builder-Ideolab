Our story

Eighteen years, one van at a time

  1. 2008

    One van, one phone

    Started with a second hand van and a landline in the spare room. Every job came from somebody telling somebody else.

  2. 2013

    The first workshop

    A unit on the industrial estate meant parts on the shelf instead of a trip to the merchant halfway through every job.

  3. 2017

    Four engineers, one standard

    Grew the team without changing how the work is done. Everybody trains in house before they are let near a customer's home.

  4. 2021

    Fixed pricing across the board

    Stopped charging by the day. Every job quoted in writing, so nobody has to wonder what the final number will be.

  5. Today

    Eleven people, same promise

    Still answering our own phone, and still finishing on the day we said we would.
