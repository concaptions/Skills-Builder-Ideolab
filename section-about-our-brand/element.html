About us

We started because nobody turned up

In 2008 the founder waited three weeks for a plumber who never came, fixed the leak himself, and decided that turning up on the day you said you would was a low enough bar to build a business on.

Eighteen years later there are eleven of us. The van is newer and the workshop is bigger, but the rule has not changed: we say a date, and we are there on it.

We still answer our own phone. If you call at four on a Friday, somebody who knows your job picks up.
